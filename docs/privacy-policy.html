Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Quick Summary: Meow Meow does not require an account. Your cat photos and saved cat data stay on your device unless you choose to send something with Cat Mail. We use limited, anonymous analytics and advertising-measurement data as described below.

1. Information We Collect

Meow Meow does not require an account, login, name, or email address. We use Mixpanel for product analytics and the Meta (Facebook) SDK for app events and advertising measurement. These services receive app-usage events and a random anonymous analytics ID. We do not send them names, email addresses, cat photos, free-form text, links, or URLs.

Anonymous Analytics ID

The random analytics ID is stored in your iPhone Keychain. It can remain after you delete and reinstall the app, and the app does not currently provide a way to reset it. It is not tied to an account or directly linked to your name or email address.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Precise or coarse location data
  • Your cat photos or other personal files (except a photo you choose to send with Cat Mail, described in section 4)
  • Payment card or other payment details

2. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking Choices

Mixpanel processes anonymous app-usage events to help us understand how the app and widgets are used. Meta processes limited app events, including events that may be automatically logged for purchases, for app-event and advertising measurement. The Meta SDK receives the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

You can change your Meta tracking choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your Apple device. Allowing tracking enables Meta's use of the advertising identifier for measurement; declining does not prevent you from using the app.

3. Photos, Cat Data, and Gift Links

Photos of your own cat, your saved cat data, and your preferences stay on your device. The app uses an App Group to share the necessary data with its widgets. App-local data is removed when you delete the app, although iOS may retain App Group data while a related widget remains installed.

If you create a gift link, the link carries only a cat ID and, if you choose to include it, a first name. Gift links are not stored on our server. A random gift ID may be used in anonymous analytics to measure whether a gift is opened or redeemed.

Stickers, care logs, and on-device AI

Stickers you make from your photos, care logs (such as feeding, litter, medicine, vet visits, and weight), reminders, and memorial settings are created and stored on your device. Optional AI features (such as cat "thoughts", name ideas, bios, and sticker captions) use Apple's on-device models; your photos and text are not sent to us or to an AI provider. When you export stickers to another app (for example WhatsApp or Telegram), that app's own privacy policy applies to what you send.

4. Cat Mail

Cat Mail lets you send a cat, a sticker, or a photo you choose, with an optional short note (up to 60 characters), to friends you have paired with by sharing a one-time invite link. To deliver it, the item you send and a random device identifier are stored on our Cloudflare server. There are no accounts, phone numbers, contact lists, public profiles, or location data.

  • Messages and photos are deleted automatically after 30 days; invites expire after 7 days.
  • You can delete a received message, unpair a friend, or block and report a sender at any time; reporting removes that sender from your friends.
  • We do not sell Cat Mail content or use it for advertising or analytics. Anonymous analytics records only that a Cat Mail action happened, not its content.
  • Do not send photos of people or anything you do not have the right to share.

5. Purchases

Meow Meow Plus is available as an auto-renewing subscription and as a lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app provides Apple an anonymous purchase token (appAccountToken) that is the same random analytics ID so Apple subscription notifications can be matched without a name or account.

6. Third-Party and Hosted Services

We use the following services to provide and improve Meow Meow:

  • Mixpanel: anonymous product analytics. See Mixpanel's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Facebook): app-event and advertising measurement. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: payment processing and StoreKit purchase services.
  • The Cat API: public cat images used by the app. See The Cat API Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosts public live-ops content and gift pages, and stores Cat Mail messages for up to 30 days to deliver them. No accounts are required.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or contact details from children. Cat Mail only works between people who have exchanged a private invite link, short notes are limited and filtered, and any recipient can block or report a sender.

8. Data Security

  • All image downloads use secure HTTPS connections
  • Your cat photos and saved cat data remain on your device unless you send one with Cat Mail, which is encrypted in transit and deleted after 30 days
  • Payment details are handled by Apple, not by us

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Please check this page periodically for updates.

10. Your Choices

  • Change the Meta tracking permission in your Apple device settings
  • Delete the app to remove its app-local data and cached content
  • Remove widgets from your home screen at any time
  • Delete Cat Mail messages, unpair friends, or block and report senders inside the app
  • Turn off AI text features in the app's Settings
